Privacy Policy

Tiny Habit Tracker

Last Updated: November 13, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Tiny Habit Tracker. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data Stored Locally

Tiny Habit Tracker is designed with privacy in mind. All your habit tracking data is stored locally on your device, including:

  • Habit names and descriptions
  • Completion records and streaks
  • Custom categories and tags
  • App preferences and settings

2.2 Data We Don't Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal information on external servers. Your data remains entirely on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your habit tracking data is used solely to provide app functionality
  • No data is shared with third parties
  • No data is used for advertising or analytics
  • No user accounts or cloud synchronization are required

4. Data Security

Your data security is important to us:

  • All data is stored locally using secure device storage
  • We use Android's built-in security features to protect your data
  • No internet connection is required for core functionality
  • Your data is only accessible through your device

5. Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Storage: To save and retrieve your habit data locally
  • Notifications: To send you habit reminders (optional)

These permissions are used solely for the app's functionality and not for data collection.

6. Third-Party Services

Tiny Habit Tracker does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks. Your data never leaves your device.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since all data is stored locally and we don't collect any information, the app is safe for users of all ages.

8. Data Backup and Export

Users can:

  • Back up their data manually through the app's export feature
  • Delete all app data by uninstalling the application
  • Clear data through device settings at any time

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
